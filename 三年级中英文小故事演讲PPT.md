## 第2页 - 故事开始

### 中文故事：
小蜗牛慢慢爬，它的朋友们都说它太慢了。

### 英文翻译：
Little snail crawls slowly. All its friends say it is too slow.

**🐌 图片生成提示词：**

## 第3页 - 故事发展

### 中文故事：
但小蜗牛不放弃，它每天努力练习，从不偷懒。

### 英文翻译：
But little snail never gives up. It practices every day and never gets lazy.

**🌱 图片生成提示词：**
> A determined cute cartoon snail practicing climbing a colorful flower stem, morning sunshine, soft pastel background, children's book illustration style, encouraging and positive mood, warm pastel colors

---

## 第4页 - 困难时刻

### 中文故事：
有一天，下起了大雨，朋友们都躲起来了，只有小蜗牛还在努力爬。

### 英文翻译：
One day, it rains hard. All the friends hide. Only little snail keeps crawling.

**🌧️ 图片生成提示词：**
> A brave little cartoon snail crawling in gentle rain, its friends peeking from a mushroom umbrella, soft gray rainy background with warm tones, cute and touching illustration, children's storybook style, pastel colors

---

## 第5页 - 转机出现

### 中文故事：
小蜗牛发现了一颗快要被雨水冲走的小花，它用身体护住了小花。

### 英文翻译：
Little snail finds a small flower about to be washed away by rain. It protects the flower with its body.

**🌸 图片生成提示词：**
> A kind cartoon snail protecting a tiny pink flower from rain with its shell, soft rain drops around, heartwarming scene, pastel colors with rainbow hint, disney style children's illustration, gentle and loving atmosphere

---

## 第6页 - 朋友们感动

### 中文故事：
朋友们看到后，都跑来帮忙，它们一起把小花种好了。

### 英文翻译：
When friends see this, they all come to help. Together, they plant the little flower safely.

**👫 图片生成提示词：**
> Happy cartoon animal friends (a bunny, a bear cub, a duck) helping plant a flower together with a little snail, sunny scene after rain, puddle reflections, joyful and warm illustration, pastel rainbow colors, pixar-style children's animation

---

## 第7页 - 故事寓意

### 中文故事：
从此，大家都说，小蜗牛虽然爬得慢，但它是最勇敢、最善良的！

### 英文翻译：
From then on, everyone says little snail crawls slowly, but it is the bravest and kindest!

**⭐ 图片生成提示词：**
> A proud happy little snail with a shiny gold medal, surrounded by smiling animal friends under a rainbow, sunny celebration scene, warm pastel colors, triumphant and uplifting mood, pixar-style children's animation illustration

---

## 第8页 - 结尾页

### 演讲结束语：

### 中文：
"谢谢大家！"
我的故事告诉我们：慢慢来，不放弃，每个人都有自己的闪光点！

### 英文：
"Thank you, everyone!"
My story tells us: Take your time, never give up. Everyone has their own shine!

**🎉 图片生成提示词：**
> A cheerful cartoon child waving at audience with confetti and stars around, stage spotlight, warm celebratory atmosphere, pastel colors, pixar-style animation, encouraging and joyful mood

## 📝 PPT制作说明

- 每页建议：标题放上方，故事文字放中间，图片放右侧或下方
- 背景色建议：浅蓝、浅粉、浅黄等柔和色调
- 字体建议：标题用圆润可爱字体，正文用清晰易读的字体
- 每页演讲时间建议：30-45秒
- 总演讲时长：约4-5分钟
