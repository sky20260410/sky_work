## Useful Sentences

- Dolphins are intelligent and social marine mammals.
  - 海豚是聪明而且合群的海洋哺乳动物。

- Dolphins usually live in groups called pods.
  - 海豚通常生活在被称为 pod 的群体中。

- Dolphins mainly eat fish and squid.
  - 海豚主要吃鱼和鱿鱼。

- Dolphins use echolocation to find food.
  - 海豚利用回声定位寻找食物。

- Dolphins can communicate with each other.
  - 海豚可以彼此交流。

- Dolphins are famous for their intelligence and playful behavior.
  - 海豚因聪明和爱玩的行为而闻名。

## Learning Tip

The pronunciation guide is now shown in two ways: **IPA (International Phonetic Alphabet)** for accuracy, and a simple learner-friendly guide for easier reading.

When you write English sentences for practice, check:
1. Subject + verb agreement
2. Singular and plural nouns
3. Articles: a / an / the
4. Prepositions
5. Natural word choice

Example:

❌ Dolphins is very smart animals.

✅ Dolphins are very smart animals.
